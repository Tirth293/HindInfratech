About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Precast Construction

Welcome to Hind Infratech, your trusted partner in high-quality precast construction and comprehensive infrastructure solutions. We specialise in delivering diverse, scalable, and cutting-edge precast services designed to meet the evolving demands of modern civil engineering and construction.

At Hind Infratech, we combine technical expertise with operational excellence to offer seamless, end-to-end services — from conceptualisation to final execution, ensuring timely delivery and compliance with the highest industry standards.

Our Services

Comprehensive Infrastructure Solutions

End-to-end precast and civil construction services with precision engineering and state-of-the-art technology.

Precast Production

Manufacturing premium precast elements — utility ducts, box culverts, boundary walls, benches, light poles, and drainage systems with guaranteed durability.

Turnkey Project Execution

Complete end-to-end project management from conceptualisation to handover, ensuring industry standard compliance and on-time delivery.

Utility Ducts & Drains

Casting and laying 600mm–1200mm utility ducts, U-shape drains, and drainage systems for large infrastructure corridors across cities.

Precast Box Culverts , Girders & Structures

Casting and erecting box culverts, protection walls,Precast girders, kerbs, coping, chambers and structural elements for road and highway infrastructure projects.

Landscaping & Flooring

Flooring, tiling, and landscaping works for dams, riverfronts, and urban public spaces — aesthetic finesse meets structural precision.

Road Furnishings

Road furnishings for national expressways and state highway projects.
